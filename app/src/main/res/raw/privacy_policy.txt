# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 10, 2026

Tracker Android is developed by Adsamcik and provided as an open-source, privacy-first app.

## Overview

Tracker Android works entirely offline. The app does not use a backend, does not sync data to remote servers, and does not use Firebase, analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, telemetry, or other third-party services to collect user data.

## Information Collection and Use

Tracker Android does not collect personal information from your device and does not transmit your tracking data anywhere.

Data created by the app stays on your device. This can include:

* location history, routes, and trip/session records
* activity recognition and step-related data
* app settings and local app data
* local crash logs that remain on-device unless you choose to export them

## Network Use

Tracker Android is designed to operate fully offline. The app does not make network calls for tracking, analytics, crash reporting, or remote storage.

## Permissions

### Location

Used for recording routes, showing your position on the map, and generating location-based statistics.

### Activity Recognition

Used for activity detection, step counting, and related on-device insights.

### Notifications

Used to display foreground service and tracking status notifications while tracking is active.

### Files and Storage Access

Used only when you choose to import or export data through Android's system file picker.

## Exports

Exports are created only when you explicitly request them. Tracker Android can export your data in formats such as GPX, KML, and JSON. Exported data goes only to the file location or app you choose.

## No Ads or Tracking

Tracker Android does not show ads and does not include ad networks, tracking pixels, analytics, telemetry, or remote crash reporting.

## Data Security

Your tracking data remains on your device unless you decide to export or share it. If you export data, the privacy of the exported copy depends on where you save or send it.

## Children’s Privacy

Tracker Android does not knowingly collect personal information from children because the app does not collect personal information from its users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the version included with the app and in the repository.
